The desk today

A notebook holds one line per item: what it is, and who has it. Ten items, about a hundred borrowers.

Availability is not written down anywhere. It is worked out by whoever is at the desk, from memory, when the next person asks.

Due dates are whatever the borrower writes in the notebook. Overdue is noticed the same way — second-hand, when someone asks for an item that is already out.

Three of the ten items are out on any given day.
