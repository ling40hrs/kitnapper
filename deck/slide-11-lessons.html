Lessons learned

Four things we would tell the next team.

“A probe suite is pressure, not proof.”

Twenty probes were green while fifteen real defects sat in the UI. The interactive walkthrough found every one of them.

“A hard line limit is a design tool.”

Two hundred lines pushed the preview panes into PreviewLayout and separate sections — and the screens got easier to change.

“Measure before you argue.”

Two open design questions closed the day the queries came back at 0.46 ms. Neither needed an architecture change.

“Write the rule down first.”

Fees, loan limits, manager navigation and scope were locked in the docs before the code. None of them drifted.

KitNapper ships as working software, with the docs that prove it.
