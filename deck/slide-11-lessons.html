Lessons learned

Four things we would tell the next team.

01

“A probe suite is pressure, not proof.”

Twenty probes were green while fifteen real defects sat in the UI.
02

“A hard line limit is a design tool.”

Two hundred lines pushed the preview panes into PreviewLayout and separate sections.
03

“Measure before you argue.”

Two open design questions closed the day the queries came back at 0.46 ms.
04

“Write the rule down first.”

Fees, limits, navigation and scope were locked in the docs before the code.

KitNapper ships as working software, with the docs that prove it.
