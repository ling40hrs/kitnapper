OOP evidence

The four pillars sit in the domain, not in the slides.

01

Abstraction and inheritance

User is abstract — Borrower and Manager, with getRole() resolved by dispatch. Equipment splits into three kinds.

02

Polymorphism

The fee rule is an interface, not a branch. A second rule would be a new class, not a new switch.

03

Encapsulation

Private fields, named transitions. The model refuses a fourth active loan, a date past fourteen days, a second settlement.

04

State as types

Roles, categories, statuses and penalty types are enums. Illegal transitions throw instead of writing a bad row.

Every rule that could be argued about lives in model/ — and the probes hold it there.
