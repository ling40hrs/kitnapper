Not built, and not caught

Left out on purpose

  • Condition grades and damage fees — a grade without photos at both ends is a guess.
  • Cancelling a loan, or moving a due date — the desk returns the item and lends it again.
  • Accounts beyond the three demo ones; no network, no sync between machines.

Missed by the tests, found by hand

  • A dropdown arrow that drew as an empty square on the dark theme.
  • Catalog photos painting over the page header while the cards view scrolled.
  • A checkout message that cut off the due date mid-sentence.
  • A status bar reporting a selection count from a screen you were not on.

Twenty probes were passing when the list on the right was still in the build.
