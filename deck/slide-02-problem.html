The problem

A paper sign-out sheet records what left the room.
It knows nothing about what is still there.

A small equipment desk runs on a clipboard: a name, an item, a scribbled date. Everything a lender actually needs has to be reconstructed by hand.

01  Availability

What is out is written down; what is free is guesswork. Two people can promise the same camera to two students, and nobody finds out until they both arrive.

02  Due dates

Nothing stops a fifteen-day loan or a return nobody notices. Overdue is discovered when the next borrower asks for the gear, not when the date passes.

03  Fees

Late fees are arithmetic someone does from memory. Nobody can say what was owed on which day, so disputes end in goodwill instead of in records.

KitNapper keeps the sheet — and the state the sheet never had.
