The problem

01

Availability

What is out is written down. What is free is guesswork, so two people can promise the same camera.

Ten items, three on loan — nothing on the sheet says which seven are free.
02

Due dates

Nothing stops a fifteen-day loan, and overdue is found when the next borrower asks.

The desk has no clock: a late item is discovered, never announced.
03
